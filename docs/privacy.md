# Privacy decisions

NeighborLift is designed around the idea that discovering support should not require publishing a precise location or unnecessary personal detail.

## Approximate areas only

Users enter a neighborhood-level area such as a district or a landmark-adjacent description. The map derives a stable approximate point from that context. Exact addresses are not requested for map placement and are never plotted. If an entry lacks usable approximate context, it remains visible in the activity list without a pin.

## Human choice stays central

AI matching ranks and explains potentially compatible posts, but people decide whether to coordinate. The interface describes the result as a suggestion and exposes the factors considered rather than presenting an unexplained automated decision.

## Optional accessibility context

Accessibility notes are optional and are intended only for information that makes support more useful or comfortable. The form encourages users to share task-relevant preferences rather than private details.

## Honest prototype data

Visible demo posts are labeled as examples. The project does not fabricate reviews, ratings, testimonials, usage numbers, or impact claims. Future impact reporting should be based on authentic participation and consent-aware measurement.
